\chapter*{Abstract}

In this project thesis, the construction of collective variables (CVs) using principal component analysis (PCA) is examined in order to drive molecular dynamics simulations (MDs) of a chemical reaction.
First, the method to construct the CVs is described. 
Then, the method is implemented for a specific system: the saponification of ethyl acetate with hydroxide in water. 
During a simulation of the reaction biased with physics-based handcrafted CVs, the reaction path is analyzed in the PCA space and the constructed CVs are observed. 
Finally, the reaction is biased with the constructed CVs and the results of the two simulations are compared to evaluate the effectiveness of the constructed CVs.
